# Next-Gen Autonomous Intralogistics & Industry 5.0 — 50 Commercial Stock Prompts

> **Format:** 16:9 Widescreen (`--ar 16:9`)  
> **Target Category:** Adobe Stock Category 11 (Industry) / Category 3 (Business & Technology)  
> **Human Presence:** Exactly 0 (Fully Unmanned / Non-Human)  
> **Upscale Profile:** Optimized for artifact-free neural upscaling (smooth matte finishes, clean continuous lines, broad specular highlights, zero micro-mesh, zero hallucinated pseudo-lettering).

---

## Technical Directive Embedded in Every Prompt

To prevent upscaler failure modes (such as the checkerboard artifacts, edge ringing, or Laplacian variance cliffs seen in dense micro-textures), every prompt adheres to these rendering rules:
1. **No Fine Micro-Mesh or Grates:** Industrial walkways and shelves use solid brushed aluminum, galvanized sheet metal, or opaque structural composite plates.
2. **No Volumetric Noise / Smoke / Dust Particles:** Lighting uses clean, diffused high-bay LED illumination or sharp planar optical scan sheets rather than foggy/dusty atmospheric particles that turn into worms during neural upscaling.
3. **No Small Text or Typography:** All containers, chassis, and screens display abstract geometric icons, solid telemetry curves, or clean solid-colored surfaces.
4. **Completely Unmanned:** No people, hands, faces, silhouettes, or reflections of humans.
